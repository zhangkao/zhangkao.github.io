Kao Zhang

Kao Zhang is a Lecturer and Master’s Supervisor at Nanjing University of Information Science and Technology (NUIST), where he serves as Head of the Department of Artificial Intelligence.

His research focuses on visual attention, image and video processing, remote sensing, and virtual reality. His work explores how machines can understand human attention, interpret complex visual scenes, and learn from multimodal information.

He received his Ph.D. from Wuhan University in 2020, supervised by Prof. Zhenzhong Chen. He also earned his B.Eng. (2014) and M.Eng. (2016) at Wuhan University under Prof. Jian Yao. Previously, he was a postdoctoral fellow at Wuhan University, a visiting researcher at Tencent, and a visiting student with the PERCEPT team at INRIA, Rennes.
